About SafeOut
WHO WE ARE · HOW IT WORKS
Who we are
SafeOut

SafeOut is a free safety tool available wherever it's needed — public spaces, transportation hubs, hotels, events, or anywhere people may be vulnerable. Our first priority is combating human trafficking.

We place QR codes in accessible locations. Anyone who feels unsafe can scan and reach help instantly — no app download, no account, no sign-up required.

Every alert is received by a real, trained person. We work with NGOs, volunteers, and authorities to make sure no scan goes unanswered.

How each button works
Get me out
Alerts trained staff that someone needs a discreet exit. A person will come to you, use a code phrase, and escort you safely out — no questions asked, no scene made.
Call somebody
Sends an automatic alert with your current location to a trusted contact you select. They receive a message that you need them to call you immediately — no explanation needed on your end.
112 — I'm in danger
Sends your precise GPS location, venue ID, and a timestamped metadata package to emergency services and our dispatch center simultaneously. Use this if you are in immediate danger or suspect human trafficking.
