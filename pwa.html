About SafeOut
WHO WE ARE · HOW IT WORKS
Who we are
SafeOut

SafeOut is a free safety tool available wherever it's needed — public spaces, transport hubs, hotels, events, or anywhere people may be vulnerable. Our first priority is combating human trafficking.

We place QR codes in accessible locations. Anyone who feels unsafe can scan and reach help instantly — no app, no account, no sign-up required.

Every alert is received by a real, trained person. We work with NGOs, volunteers, and authorities to make sure no scan goes unanswered.

How each button works
Get me out
Alerts trained staff that someone needs a discreet exit. A person will come to you, use a code phrase, and escort you safely out — no questions asked.
Call somebody
Sends an automatic alert with your location to a trusted contact. They receive a message that you need them to call you immediately.
112 — I'm in danger
Sends your GPS location, venue ID, and timestamped metadata to emergency services and our dispatch center simultaneously. Use when in immediate danger or if you suspect human trafficking.
